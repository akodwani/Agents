# Film QC Rubric

**Apply to the assembled cut before it becomes a portfolio piece.**
Run it twice: once at the rough-cut stage (Day 20 morning) and once on the master (Day 20 end).

## Scoring
Each criterion 0–5. **Threshold: total ≥ 75/100 AND no individual criterion below 3.**
A single 2 anywhere means the film is not ready, regardless of total — because one visible
failure is what a viewer remembers.

---

## A. STORY AND STRUCTURE (20)

| # | Criterion | 5 = | 2 = |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Story clarity** | A stranger describes what happened, correctly, with no explanation from you | They ask "so what was that?" |
| A2 | **Beginning / escalation / payoff** | Three distinct movements are visible | It is a mood reel |
| A3 | **Objective is legible** | You can see what the subject wants | The subject just exists |
| A4 | **Payoff lands** | The final beat delivers on the setup | It stops rather than ends |

**A1 is the highest-stakes test in this document.** Show it to one person who knows nothing.
Say nothing. Ask them afterwards what happened. Their answer is your score.

## B. SPATIAL COHERENCE (20)

| # | Criterion | 5 = | 2 = |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | **Spatial continuity** | Every shot occupies the same believable space | Shots feel like different locations |
| B2 | **Screen direction** | Consistent throughout; any deliberate break is motivated | Subject randomly reverses direction |
| B3 | **180° line respected** | Never crossed, or crossed with an explicit neutral shot | Crossed carelessly |
| B4 | **Geography establishes early** | The audience knows where things are by shot 2–3 | Never established |

## C. CAMERA AND CRAFT (20)

| # | Criterion | 5 = | 2 = |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Shot hierarchy** | Clear wide/medium/close structure serving the beats | All shots the same size |
| C2 | **Lens progression** | Deliberate and defensible | Random focal lengths |
| C3 | **Camera grammar** | Every move has a reason you can state | Everything drifts for no reason |
| C4 | **Repetition avoided** | No two shots feel interchangeable | Several near-duplicates |

**C3 note:** at least two shots in the final film must be **locked off**. Constant motion is the
most common tell of an inexperienced AI filmmaker; stillness reads as confidence.

## D. EDIT AND SOUND (20)

| # | Criterion | 5 = | 2 = |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | **Pacing** | Rhythm supports escalation; the payoff has room | Even, flat, or arbitrary |
| D2 | **Cut quality** | Cuts land on action or on a motivated beat | Cuts feel like clip boundaries |
| D3 | **Sound design** | Continuous ambience + designed effects + music | Music only, or silence |
| D4 | **Mix** | ~-14 LUFS integrated, true peak < -1 dBTP, nothing masks anything | Clipping, or effects buried |

**D3 minimum bar:** continuous ambience bed across the whole piece, at least 5 designed hard
effects, and a sub-bass element on the payoff. This is not optional — sound is roughly 30% of
perceived quality for 10% of the time.

## E. FINISH (20)

| # | Criterion | 5 = | 2 = |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | **Compositing quality** | Composited elements are indistinguishable from the plate | Visible pasting, halos |
| E2 | **Colour consistency** | All shots read as one camera, one day, one grade | Shots visibly mismatched |
| E3 | **Grain / texture consistency** | Uniform across the piece | Some shots clean, some noisy |
| E4 | **Technical delivery** | Correct fps, resolution, no banding, clean export | Stutter, banding, wrong AR |

---

## THE AI-SLOP CHECKLIST — automatic fail if 3+ are true

These are the specific things that make a viewer discount your work in the first four seconds.

- [ ] Every shot is slow-motion or drifting — nothing is ever still
- [ ] Every shot is the same size and roughly the same length
- [ ] Excessive lens flare, volumetric god-rays, or bloom on every shot
- [ ] Hyper-saturated orange/teal grade applied uniformly
- [ ] Subject changes appearance between shots
- [ ] No coherent geography — you cannot draw a floor plan of the scene
- [ ] No sound design (music-only bed)
- [ ] Shots feel like a montage of unrelated pretty images
- [ ] Camera moves have no motivation
- [ ] The piece has no ending — it just stops
- [ ] Text/UI/signage in frame is garbled
- [ ] Physically impossible motion nobody addressed
