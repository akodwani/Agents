# Current Role Gap Analysis

Read this on Day 21 after scoring the final exam. It maps your score to specific actions.

---

## If you scored 80+ — apply now

**Apply within 48 hours.** The work is freshest in your head and perfectionism has not set in.

**Target order:**
1. **AI-native studios** (Wonder Studios, Promise, Staircase, Latent Space Media, Leonine) — they
   care most about the reel and least about credentials
2. **Agency AI roles** (Publicis, Monks, Code and Theory, Superside, WPP Production) — lead with
   the pipeline breakdown and the Console, not the film
3. **Product/platform** (Lightricks-type companies) — lead with the Console and the API work
4. **Freelance / contract** (Ksar Production-type listings, Curious Refuge board) — fastest route
   to a first credit, and a credit changes every subsequent conversation

**Application asymmetry that matters:** for studios, the *film* is the application. For agencies,
the *breakdown* is. Do not send the same email to both.

## If you scored 65–79 — the highest-yield 10–20 hours

Find your two lowest-scoring competencies and fix only those.

| If your gap is | Do exactly this | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| **#13 Comfy workflows (8 pts)** | Rebuild both workflows cleanly, export JSON, write both READMEs, produce the strength sweep | 6 |

**Start with sound (#11) and explanation (#14).** They are the cheapest points per hour and both
raise your Film QC and interview performance simultaneously.

## If you scored 50–64

Usually this means the film shipped but the *system* did not. The points are almost always in:
- Missing or incomplete **Shot QC logs** (#7, #8)
- No **workflow JSON or READMEs** (#13)
- A **breakdown that is a highlight reel** rather than a process document (#14)

None of that requires new generation. It requires 8–12 hours of documenting what you already did.
**This is the best hours-to-points ratio available anywhere in this course** — go do it before
anything else.

## The 60-day plan after Day 21

| Weeks | Focus |
|---|---|
| **1–2** | **Apply.** 20–30 applications to READY families. Post the 5 supporting artifacts (ladder, previs→final, strength sweep, AE toggle, Console) one at a time |
| **3–4** | **Second piece.** A different genre, reusing the whole pipeline. It should take ~25 hours now, not 105. **Two pieces is the credibility threshold — one can be luck** |
| **5–6** | **Close your top gap** from the table above |
| **7–8** | **Third piece or first paid work.** Take freelance even if underpaid — a credit changes every subsequent conversation |

## The single most important thing

**Two finished pieces beats one perfect piece.** The most common failure after a course like this
is polishing piece one for two months. Ship it, apply, and start piece two — the second one will
be better than any amount of polishing could have made the first.
