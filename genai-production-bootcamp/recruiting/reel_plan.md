# Reel Plan

## What the reel is for

A reel is not a film. **It is a filter.** A hiring manager watches 6 seconds before deciding
whether to watch the rest. The reel's only job is to buy the next 20 seconds.

## The 20–30 second cut

| Time | Content | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:04** | **Your strongest 2 shots, cut hard.** No title card, no logo, no fade-in | Survive the 6-second filter |
| 0:04–0:12 | The escalation — 3–4 shots showing range of size and motion | Prove it wasn't one lucky shot |
| 0:12–0:22 | The payoff shot, held longer than expected | Show you can land a beat |
| 0:22–0:28 | One AE before/after or the previs→final wipe | **Prove there is a process** |
| 0:28–0:30 | Name + role + contact. Two seconds, static | Be findable |

**Open on your best frame. Never on a title card.** A title card is 3 seconds of your 6-second
budget spent on something nobody wants.

## Hard rules

1. **No music-only reel.** Sound design must be audible — ambience, effects, sub-bass. A
   music-only bed is the most common reel mistake and it reads as hiding behind the track.
2. **No slow fades between every shot.** Cut hard. Fades read as padding.
3. **Never include a shot you are not proud of** to hit a duration. 22 strong seconds beats 30
   with 8 weak ones.
4. **Include the process moment.** The previs→final or before/after wipe is what separates you
   from the thousands of people posting generations.
5. **Same colour and grain treatment throughout.** A reel that visibly mismatches shot to shot
   undermines the claim that you can finish.
6. **Export at 1080p minimum, 24fps, high bitrate.** A compressed reel makes good work look bad.

## Versions to produce

| Version | Spec | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Master reel** | 20–30s, 16:9, 1080p+ | Portfolio site, email, applications |
| **Vertical cut** | 9:16, **re-framed per shot, not cropped** | Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn video |
| **The full film** | 30–60s, 16:9 | Portfolio page, linked from the reel |
| **Silent-safe cut** | Master with burned captions on the process moment | Autoplay-muted feeds |

Re-framing for vertical means repositioning the subject per shot. A centre-crop of a wide shot
throws away the composition you spent 21 days learning to build.

## Where it goes

| Platform | Note |
|---|---|
| **Portfolio site** | The canonical link. Everything else points here |
| **LinkedIn** | Post the vertical with a **written breakdown in the caption** — the technical detail is what recruiters engage with, not the video |
| **Instagram / TikTok** | Vertical. Optional, and explicitly *not* the priority — you are not building a creator audience, you are getting hired |
| **Direct applications** | Link the portfolio page, never a raw file |

## What to post alongside it

The reel alone is one artifact. Post these separately over the following weeks — they perform
better with a technical audience than the reel does:

1. **The control-ladder comparison** (rung 0 vs rung 2 vs rung 4)
2. **The previs→final** side by side
3. **The ComfyUI strength sweep**
4. **The AE integration toggle demo**
5. **The Console** with its problem statement

Each of these is a small, specific, technically legible proof. Collectively they build a
reputation for *method*, which is what gets you referred.
