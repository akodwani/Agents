# DAY 12 — Depth, Normal and Mask Passes + The Motion Driver

**TIME BUDGET: 5h** — 0.5h watch · 0.5h read · 3.5h do · 0.5h QC

## OUTCOME
A complete utility-pass set (depth EXR + normalized PNG, normal, object-index mask) and an
encoded camera-move clip ready to drive a video model.

## WHY THIS MATTERS
The depth pass is what makes ComfyUI Workflow A possible tomorrow. The camera-move clip is what
makes your two difficult motion shots possible on Day 17. Today produces the raw material for
both of the final film's hardest technique requirements.

## LEARN
- Render passes: Z depth, normal, object index
- Why depth must be 32-bit EXR (never quantise a control signal)
- **The ControlNet depth convention: near = white.** Getting this backwards makes your control
  image silently do nothing
- Easing a camera move — a linear dolly reads as robotic and the video model will copy that
- ffmpeg encoding for use as `video_references`

Do not treat a Short as a teaching source — it cannot be reproducible.
Otherwise: no video today. Reps.

## READ
- `modules/blender-ai-previs.md` §3–§4 — 20 min
- `modules/comfyui.md` §3 Workflow A — 10 min (so you know what the depth pass is *for*)

## DO
1. **(45 min)** Enable passes: `use_pass_z`, `use_pass_normal`, `use_pass_object_index`. Set
   `pass_index` on your objects (hero 10, foreground 20, background 30). Wire the compositor —
   `previs_rig.py` does this in `build_compositor()`; read that function and understand it.
2. **(45 min)** Render the full pass set from all three cameras. Inspect the depth PNG: **near
   should be white.** Confirm there is visible near/mid/far separation.
3. **(60 min) Animate the camera move.** A 3–5 second dolly, orbit or track. **Ease it** —
   bezier, ease-in-out, not linear. Render the image sequence.
4. **(30 min)** Encode it:
   ```bash
   ffmpeg -framerate 24 -i previs_out/move/dolly_%04d.png \
          -c:v libx264 -pix_fmt yuv420p -crf 18 previs_out/move/dolly_ref.mp4
   ```
5. **(30 min)** First bridge test: pass `dolly_ref.mp4` as `video_references` to `seedance_2_5`
   (`mode: omni_reference`). One generation, 480p. **Does the model follow your camera?**
6. **(30 min)** Log the result honestly, including if it didn't work.

## FAILURE CONDITIONS
- **Depth map is flat white or flat black** → the Normalize node is missing, or your scene has
  no depth range. Add or move the foreground occluder.
- **Depth appears inverted** → check the Invert node. Near = white is the convention.
- **Camera move is robotic** → keyframe interpolation is linear. Set BEZIER + EASE_IN_OUT.
  The video model will faithfully reproduce robotic motion.
- **Model ignores `video_references`** → confirm the model exposes that role. `seedance_2_5`,
  `wan3_0`, `minimax_h3`, `flux_3_video` and `seedance_2_0` do. `veo3_1` does not.

## RECOVERY
If compositor output nodes confuse you: render passes to a multilayer EXR instead
(`scene.render.image_settings.file_format = 'OPEN_EXR_MULTILAYER'`) and extract in ComfyUI or AE.
Fewer moving parts.

## STOP RULE
Stop when you have a depth pass you would trust as a ControlNet input and a move clip you would
trust as a motion reference. Test both tomorrow.
