# DAY 21 — Console and the Recruiting Package

**TIME BUDGET: 5h** — 0h watch · 0h read · 4h do · 1h final exam

## OUTCOME
The Shot Control Console finished, and the complete recruiting package assembled. **Course done.**

## WHY THIS MATTERS
A film alone gets you into the AI-native studio conversation. The film **plus** a production
breakdown **plus** a tool you built gets you into the agency and product conversations too —
which is three times the market. Today is the difference between one target and three.

## DO
1. **(150 min) TOOL TRACK — Console v5 (final).**
   - FastAPI wrapper over the existing CLI (`/shots`, `/shots/{id}`, `/shots/{id}/prompt`,
     `/manifest`). Use `/docs` as your test UI.
   - Minimal React/TS front end — a table of shots, a detail view, a version diff. **~90 minutes.
     Do not style it.** You already know this stack; this should be the easy part.
   - One live generation API call working end to end (Higgsfield CLI/Skills, or Comfy Cloud if
     you took the subscription).
   - **The README** — the actual deliverable. Under 400 words, answering in this order:
     (1) what production problem this solves, (2) why a schema is the right answer,
     (3) what its limits are. **Stating limits clearly reads as senior; overclaiming reads as junior.**
2. **(90 min) Portfolio breakdown.** Use `recruiting/portfolio_case_study_template.md`. Assemble:
   idea → boards → previs (clay/depth) → hero frames → Comfy workflow + strength sweep →
   Higgsfield pipeline and control rungs → **AE before/after** → Premiere timeline screenshot →
   **failures and abandonments** → final.
   **Include the failures.** Every experienced reviewer will recognise them, and hiding them
   makes you look inexperienced rather than flawless.
3. **(60 min) Recruiting assembly.** Fill `recruiting/readiness_matrix.md` with your actual
   evidence. Build the reel per `recruiting/reel_plan.md`. Draft answers to
   `recruiting/interview_questions.md` §1–§5 — out loud, not just written.

## THE FINAL EXAM (1h)
Take `recruiting/readiness_matrix.md` §Final Practical Assessment. Score yourself honestly
against all 16 competencies. **80+ = recruiting ready for junior/all-level applications.**

## VERIFY

- [ ] `console manifest` output matches what you actually shot
- [ ] `console diff` shows a real change you made during production

- [ ] Portfolio breakdown complete **including the failures section**

- [ ] Final exam scored honestly

## FAILURE CONDITIONS
- **The Console eats the whole day** → time-box at 150 minutes. **The README matters more than
  the UI.** A CLI with an excellent README beats a pretty UI with none.
- **The breakdown is a highlight reel** → then it is not a breakdown. It must show process,
  including what failed. That is the entire value of the document.
- **You score below 80** → read `recruiting/current-role-gap-analysis.md`. It tells you exactly
  which 10–20 hours close the specific gaps, and which role families are already open to you at
  your current score. Apply anyway to the READY families — a score of 72 with a finished film
  beats a score of 90 with nothing shipped.

## STOP RULE
Stop when the package exists and the exam is scored. **Then apply to something within 48 hours,
before the work goes stale in your head and before perfectionism sets in.** The portfolio is
done when it is in front of someone, not when it is perfect.
