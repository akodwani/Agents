# DAY 17 — Generation Pass 1

**TIME BUDGET: 5h** — 0h watch · 0h read · 4h do (incl. 1h Tool Track) · 1h QC (**GATE G4**)

## OUTCOME
Shots 1–5 (roughly half the film) generated and through Shot QC.

## WHY THIS MATTERS
This is where the control system either pays off or does not. If Days 1–16 were done properly,
today is calm: most shots land in 2–4 takes. If it feels like gambling, the problem is upstream
and you should go back rather than push forward.

## LEARN
- Production rhythm: specify → generate at fast tier → QC → promote or re-specify
- Reading a QC failure as a specification error, not bad luck

## DO
1. **(60 min) TOOL TRACK — Console v4.** Generation metadata capture (model, params, seed,
   attempt, credits, QC result) and `console diff v2 v3`. **Then use it for every generation
   today** — this is what makes the production log real rather than reconstructed.
2. **(180 min) Generate shots 1–5.**
   - Iterate at **480p / `fast` or `mini` tier**, `generate_audio: false`
   - **Every shot at rung ≥2**; the assigned difficult-motion shots at rung 4 or 5
   - For the previs-controlled shot: Genjutsu (`hf_mult_motion_control`) or `video_references`
   - **Shot QC each one as it lands** — do not batch the QC to the end
   - Promote passes to 1080p+ with the identical spec
   - **Hard cap: 6 generations per shot.** At 6, apply the abandonment rule
3. **(60 min) GATE G4.** Every shot: PASS / CONDITIONAL / FAIL. Log failed criteria per attempt.
   For CONDITIONAL shots, name the specific AE fix now and record it.

## VERIFY — GATE G4
- [ ] Shots 1–5 exist and each is PASS or a CONDITIONAL with a named AE fix
- [ ] No shot exceeded 6 generations without triggering abandonment
- [ ] Every generation logged in the Console with credits
- [ ] Adjacent shots checked for screen-direction consistency **as a pair**, not individually
- [ ] The previs-controlled shot's camera actually follows the previs

## SHIP
`06_gen/s01..s05` + sidecars, generation log table, updated Console.

## FAILURE CONDITIONS
- **Shot hits 6 generations** → **abandon and re-specify.** Three legitimate exits: new start
  frame; change shot size/angle so the failing element leaves frame; cut the shot. Record which
  you chose and why — **abandonments belong in your portfolio breakdown**, they demonstrate
  judgement.
- **Contact physics fail (C5) repeatedly** → nearly unfixable by re-rolling. Reframe so contact
  is out of shot, or make the subject static.
- **Two adjacent shots break screen direction** → check whether flipping one horizontally fixes
  it. Look for asymmetric details first (text, markings, lighting direction).
- **You are re-rolling and hoping** → stop. That is the exact behaviour this course exists to
  replace. Go back to the start frame.

## RECOVERY
If more than two shots fail Gate G4: **stop generating.** The problem is in your frames, not the
model. Spend the remaining time re-authoring start frames. That will be faster than continuing.

## STOP RULE
Stop at 5 hours regardless. Day 18 has slack designed into it for exactly this.
