# DAY 20 — Edit, Sound, Colour, Master

**TIME BUDGET: 5h** — 0h watch · 0h read · 4h do · 1h QC (**GATES G5, G6**)

## OUTCOME
**The finished film.** Master exported. Reel cut exported. This is the day the work becomes a
portfolio piece.

## WHY THIS MATTERS
Everything before today was material. Today it becomes a film. Sound and pacing will move your
Film QC score more than any other five hours in the course — sound alone is roughly 30% of
perceived quality for 10% of the effort.

## DO
1. **(60 min) Assemble and cut.** All shots in order at intended length. Then cut properly:
   - shot lengths serving escalation (progressively tighter), payoff held longer
   - cut on action where it hides generation inconsistency
   - **≥3 J-cuts or L-cuts**
   - **Criterion 6 test, applied ruthlessly: is the film worse without this shot? If not, cut it.**
     A 40-second film with 7 necessary shots beats a 60-second film with 10 and 3 of padding.
2. **(30 min) GATE G5 — assembly QC.** Watch it through once. Does the story read? Naive-viewer
   test if you can get one. Do not proceed to polish on a fail — fix the cut.
3. **(75 min) Sound design.**
   - **A1: continuous ambience bed across the entire piece, first.** This is the glue that makes
     cuts stop feeling like cuts. Lay it before anything else.
   - A2: ≥5 hard effects. **Cut each 1–2 frames early** so it lands perceptually on the beat.
   - A3: design/texture — drones, risers, and **a sub-bass hit on the payoff**
   - A4: music, sitting 6–10 dB under hard effects at the climax
   - Mix to ~**-14 LUFS integrated**, true peak **< -1 dBTP**
4. **(45 min) Colour.** Reference shot on the timeline; Lumetri Comparison view; match black
   point → white point → hue on every shot. Check the Waveform and Vectorscope. Then one
   adjustment layer for the look over the whole timeline. **Generated footage is usually
   over-contrasted and over-saturated — pulling back often reads as more cinematic.**
5. **(30 min) Export.** Master (H.264, 1080p+/24fps, VBR 2-pass 20–40 Mbps, AAC 320,
   **Render at Maximum Depth ON** to avoid banding in dark areas). Then the **20–30 second reel
   cut**.
6. **(60 min) GATE G6 — Film QC.** Full rubric. Naive-viewer test. AI-slop checklist.

## VERIFY — GATES G5 & G6
- [ ] **Film QC ≥ 75/100, no individual criterion below 3**
- [ ] Fewer than 3 AI-slop flags
- [ ] A naive viewer correctly describes what happened
- [ ] ≥3 J/L cuts; ≥2 locked-off shots; ≥3 distinct shot sizes
- [ ] Continuous ambience + ≥5 hard effects + sub-bass on the payoff
- [ ] All shots colour-matched — they read as one camera, one day
- [ ] No banding in dark areas on the exported file (check the export, not the timeline)
- [ ] Master and reel cut both exported

## SHIP
`09_master/final_film_v1.mp4`, `09_master/reel_cut_v1.mp4`, completed Film QC sheet,
Premiere project.

## FAILURE CONDITIONS
- **Film QC < 75** → the fastest routes up, in order: (1) more sound design, (2) tighten the
  cut and remove a padding shot, (3) fix colour mismatch. All three are cheaper than any
  visual regeneration and score higher.
- **Naive viewer cannot say what happened** → almost always a missing establishing beat.
  Try re-ordering so geography lands in the first two shots.
- **Banding in the sky/shadows** → Render at Maximum Depth, and add ~1% grain over the whole
  piece. Grain hides banding.
- **It feels like a montage** → check ambience is truly continuous under every cut. This single
  fix converts more montages into scenes than anything else.

## RECOVERY
Short on time: prioritise **sound over colour over comp**, every time. That ordering maximises
Film QC score per remaining minute.

## STOP RULE
Stop when Film QC ≥75 and both exports exist. **You have made a film.** Tomorrow is packaging.
