# DAY 19 — After Effects Finishing

**TIME BUDGET: 5h** — 0h watch · 0h read · 4.5h do · 0.5h QC

## OUTCOME
**AE4 complete**: your hero shot finished to portfolio level, plus every CONDITIONAL shot's
scheduled fix applied.

## WHY THIS MATTERS
This is the day that makes your film look *made* rather than *generated*. It also produces the
single most valuable page of your portfolio breakdown — the AE before/after. Employers who
review reels look specifically for evidence that you can finish, because most AI reels cannot.

## LEARN
- Applying the full integration chain under time pressure
- Prioritising: which shot most repays an hour of comp?

## DO
1. **(30 min) Triage.** List every CONDITIONAL shot and its named fix. Estimate minutes each.
   **Pick the ONE hero shot** that gets the substantial composite — choose the shot that is most
   visible, most on-screen-time, or most central to the payoff. Order the rest by
   minutes-to-improvement.
2. **(150 min) EXERCISE AE4 — the hero shot.** Full chain, in order:
   - repair/patch anything the upstream chain left
   - matte (Object Matte, or your `sam_3_video` matte from Day 18)
   - composite the element(s)
   - **integrate: light wrap → colour match → defocus → motion blur → grain (last, over all)**
   - render to a high-quality intermediate (ProRes 422 HQ / DNxHR HQX)
3. **(90 min) Sweep the CONDITIONAL shots** in triage order. Time-box each. When a shot's box
   expires, ship what you have and move on.
4. **(30 min)** Export a **before/after split-screen for the hero shot.** Portfolio asset.

## VERIFY
- [ ] The hero shot passes every row of `rubrics/shot_qc.md`
- [ ] Full integration chain applied and each step demonstrably doing something (toggle test)
- [ ] All CONDITIONAL shots addressed or consciously accepted as-is with a note
- [ ] Everything rendered to intermediates and imported into the Premiere timeline
- [ ] Before/after split-screen exported

## SHIP
`07_comp/` (AE projects), `07_comp/renders/*.mov` (intermediates),
`day19/hero_before_after.mp4`.

## FAILURE CONDITIONS
- **The hero shot eats the whole day** → time-box it at 150 minutes. A good-enough hero shot
  plus five fixed shots beats one perfect shot and five unfixed ones.
- **A fix reveals a deeper problem** → note it and move on. You cannot regenerate today; that
  window closed yesterday, deliberately.
- **You start "improving" shots that already passed** → stop. Passed is passed. Spend the time
  on sound tomorrow, which buys more perceived quality per minute than more comp does.

## RECOVERY
If a comp is not working after an hour: simplify it. Fewer layers, a locked-off frame, or a
shorter on-screen duration. A shot that is on screen for 1.5 seconds does not need a perfect
matte.

## STOP RULE
Stop at 5 hours. Tomorrow is the edit, and an unedited film with perfect comps is not a film.
