# DAY 5 — SHIP: The Five-Second Study

**TIME BUDGET: 5h** — 0h watch · 0.25h read · 4h do · 0.75h QC + log

## OUTCOME
**Your first finished, portfolio-eligible artifact.** A 5–8 second shot, fully specified,
QC-passed, sound-designed and mastered.

## WHY THIS MATTERS
Five days in, you have something to show. That matters psychologically, and it matters
practically — this is your first reel beat and your first social post. It also proves the
pipeline works end to end before you commit to the final film.

## LEARN
- Sound design minimum: ambience bed + hard effects
- Loudness targets: ~-14 LUFS integrated, true peak < -1 dBTP
- The Shot QC gate as an actual gate

## WATCH
**None.** Ship day.

## READ
- `projects/five-second-study.md` (the full brief) — 10 min
- `rubrics/shot_qc.md` — 5 min

## DO
1. **(30 min)** Write the shot spec into the Console. **Before generating.** All columns.
2. **(90 min)** Author the start frame and derive the end frame. Spend the time here — this
   frame determines the shot. Iterate on the *frame*, cheaply, not on the video.
3. **(60 min)** Generate. Rung ≥ 2. Iterate at 480p fast tier. **Maximum 8 generations.**
   Promote the winner to 1080p+ with the same spec.
4. **(45 min)** Sound design in Premiere: continuous ambience bed + at least one hard effect.
   Cut the effect 1–2 frames early so it lands perceptually on the beat.
5. **(30 min)** Colour, export master (H.264 1080p+ 24fps, VBR 2-pass, 20–40 Mbps, AAC 320).
6. **(45 min)** Full Shot QC. Complete the sidecar. Log every attempt with credits spent.

## VERIFY
- [ ] Shot QC = **PASS** (not CONDITIONAL)
- [ ] ≤ 8 generations, all logged with credits
- [ ] The shot matches the written spec — read the spec aloud while watching
- [ ] Reproducible: regenerate from the sidecar; is it recognisably the same shot?
- [ ] Sound present and deliberate — **mute it and confirm the shot gets noticeably worse**
- [ ] No AE was used (that is the constraint)

## SHIP
`09_master/five_second_study_v1.mp4`, its sidecar JSON, and the generation log table.

## FAILURE CONDITIONS
- **Shot QC returns CONDITIONAL and you ship anyway** → don't. The whole point of a gate is
  that it stops you. Regenerate.
- **You hit 8 generations without a PASS** → the spec is wrong, not the model. Re-author the
  start frame, or change the shot size so the failing element is out of frame. Both are
  legitimate directorial moves; re-rolling is not.
- **Contact physics fail (C5) — feet/wheels sliding** → almost never fixable by re-rolling. Pick
  a shot where contact is not visible, or where the subject is static.

## RECOVERY
Sound sourcing: freesound.org (check licences), Adobe Stock audio if you have CC, or generate
ambience and effects with Higgsfield `generate_audio`. Do not skip sound — it is 30% of
perceived quality.

## STOP RULE
Stop when Shot QC passes and the master is exported. **Do not polish further.** You have 16 days
of harder work ahead and this is a study, not a masterpiece.
