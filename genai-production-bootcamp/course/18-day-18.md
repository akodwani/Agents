# DAY 18 — Generation Pass 2 and Repair

**TIME BUDGET: 5h** — 0h watch · 0h read · 4h do · 1h QC

## OUTCOME
**All shots generated and PASSing.** The full repair chain applied. The film exists as material.

## WHY THIS MATTERS
This day has deliberate slack in it. If Day 17 went well you will finish early and can raise
quality; if it went badly this is where you recover. Either way, by tonight you must have every
shot — because Days 19–21 are finishing, not generating.

## LEARN
- The repair chain as a routine step, not an emergency measure
- When to stop improving a shot

## DO
1. **(150 min) Generate remaining shots.** Same discipline as Day 17. Shot QC as you go.
2. **(60 min) Repair pass on ALL shots.** For every shot, in order:
   - `video_deflicker` — kills the temporal flicker that reads as "AI"
   - `bytedance_video_upscale` with **`preset: "aigc"`** — the AI-content-tuned upscaler
   - `sam_3_video` where you will need a matte in AE tomorrow (`apply_mask`)
   **This is 60 minutes that will save you many hours in AE. Do not skip it.**
3. **(30 min) Full-film screen-direction audit.** Lay every shot in order in Premiere at rough
   length. Watch once for direction only, ignoring quality. Fix or flip anything that breaks.
4. **(60 min) Final Shot QC on the complete set.** Every shot PASS or a CONDITIONAL with a
   named, scheduled AE fix.

## VERIFY
- [ ] Every shot in the film exists and is PASS or scheduled-CONDITIONAL
- [ ] Repair chain applied to all shots; before/after kept for at least one (portfolio material)
- [ ] Screen direction holds across the entire sequence, watched end to end
- [ ] All technique requirements satisfied and identifiable:
      ≥2 difficult motion · ≥1 previs-controlled · ≥1 Comfy-controlled · ≥1 AE composite planned
- [ ] Total generation count and credit spend logged

## SHIP
`06_gen/` complete + sidecars, `day18/repair_before_after.mp4`, complete generation log.

## FAILURE CONDITIONS
- **A shot still will not pass** → **cut it.** You have 6–10 shots; the film survives losing one.
  A film that ships beats a film with one perfect shot. Record the cut and the reason.
- **Repair chain makes a shot look worse** → the upscaler can over-sharpen. Try `model_version:
  standard` instead of `pro`, or skip the upscale for that shot and note why.
- **You are out of credits** → finish at 720p. Resolution is the least important variable in
  your Film QC score; continuity and sound matter far more.

## RECOVERY
If you are genuinely short on shots and time: **shorten the film.** A tight 35-second piece that
holds together scores far better on Film QC than a 60-second piece with two broken shots. The
brief's floor is 30 seconds — use it.

## STOP RULE
Stop when every shot passes and the repair chain is applied. **From tomorrow you do not generate
video.** Lock the material.
