## FAILURE CONDITIONS
- **Camera resets to origin when you add a transform track** → known community report. Set the
  first keyframe explicitly from known world coordinates rather than trusting the editor default.
  The provided script does this.
- **Python calls fail** → Sequencer Scripting plugin not enabled, or an API rename between
  versions (`add_master_track` ≤5.3 vs `add_track` ≥5.4 — the script handles both).
- **UE won't run acceptably on a 1660 SUPER** → lower the scalability settings to Low. You are
  rendering grey boxes; you do not need Lumen or Nanite.
- **You are two hours in and fighting the install** → **cut the day.** That is the correct call
  and it was pre-authorised at the top of this file.

## RECOVERY
Epic's UE 5.8 MCP exists and is **Experimental (localhost only, APIs may change)** — you may use
it to help *author* the script, but keep the `.py` as the artifact. `sam-david/unreal-mcp` is the
least invasive community option (no mandatory C++ plugin) if you want to try one.

## STOP RULE
Stop at 5 hours regardless of state. Write the verdict even if the answer is "the Blender route
gave me better control per hour" — **that is a legitimate, and frankly stronger, finding**, and
it makes a better interview answer than pretending Unreal was essential.
