# DAY 7 — Tracking, Matting and Integration

**TIME BUDGET: 5h** — 1h watch · 0.5h read · 3h do · 0.5h QC

## OUTCOME
**AE2 complete**: a generated object tracked into a plate and integrated so it sits *in* the
shot rather than on it.

## WHY THIS MATTERS
Integration is the specific craft that makes composited AI elements believable. It is also the
most demonstrable skill in this whole course — the toggle-layers-on-and-off demo is genuinely
compelling in an interview, because the improvement is undeniable and you can explain each step.

## LEARN
- Point tracking vs **planar tracking (Mocha AE)** vs 3D Camera Tracker — and when each fails
- **Object Matte** (AE 26.2+) and importing a `sam_3_video` matte
- Content-Aware Fill for Video, and the CAF + Clone Stamp hybrid that professionals actually use
- **The integration chain: light wrap → colour match → defocus → motion blur → grain**

## WATCH
Your audited AE **SECONDARY** or the tracking/integration chapters of your primary, ~45 min.
Focus on **light wrap** and **grain matching** specifically — these two techniques do most of
the work and are most often skipped by tutorials.

## READ
- `modules/after-effects.md` §2.4–§2.6 and §3 — 25 min

## DO
1. **(30 min) Matte comparison.** Take one generated clip. Pull a matte three ways:
   AE **Object Matte**; Higgsfield **`sam_3_video`** (`apply_mask`); a hand mask.
   Compare edges at 200%. **Know which is fastest and which is most accurate — they differ.**
2. **(45 min) Tracking drill.** Track a plate three ways: point track, Mocha planar, 3D camera
   track. Note which succeeds on generated footage. (3D camera solve often fails on generated
   plates — knowing *when to abandon it* is the lesson.)
3. **(105 min) EXERCISE AE2 — track and composite a generated object into a plate.**
   Generate an object with a removable background. Track a plate. Place the object. Then run the
   **full integration chain in order**: light wrap → colour match → defocus → motion blur → grain.
4. **(30 min)** Record the toggle demo: screen-capture yourself switching each integration layer
   off and on. This is a portfolio asset.

## VERIFY
- [ ] Object does not slide against the plate — scrub the whole shot frame by frame
- [ ] **Light wrap present** — toggle it and see the difference
- [ ] Black point, white point and hue matched to the plate
- [ ] Defocus and motion blur match the plate's
- [ ] Grain matched, applied last, over everything
- [ ] You can say in one sentence what each integration step bought you

## SHIP
`day07/ae2_composite.mp4`, `day07/integration_toggle_demo.mp4`, AE project.

## FAILURE CONDITIONS
- **Element still looks pasted on despite everything** → almost always missing light wrap, or a
  matte edge that is too hard. Choke the matte 1–2px and add the wrap.
- **3D camera track fails** → expected on generated footage. Don't fight it. Switch to a
  locked-off plate or a planar track. Log the finding; it is a real production insight.
- **Colour match looks right in isolation, wrong in the cut** → match against the *adjacent
  shots*, not just the plate.

## RECOVERY
If Object Matte gives poor edges on your subject, `sam_3_video` sometimes wins and vice versa —
they use different models. Trying the other one takes two minutes.

## STOP RULE
Stop when the toggle demo makes the case by itself — when switching light wrap off makes an
obvious difference to someone who does not know what light wrap is.
