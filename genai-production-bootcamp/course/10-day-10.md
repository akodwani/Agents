# DAY 10 — SHIP: The Controlled Micro-Scene

**TIME BUDGET: 5h** — 0h watch · 0.25h read · 4h do · 0.75h QC

## OUTCOME
**A finished 10–20 second, 3–4 shot scene** — your first work that is a *scene* rather than a shot.

## WHY THIS MATTERS
This is the dress rehearsal for the final film. Every failure mode you hit today is one you will
not hit on Day 17, when it would cost far more. A 10–20 second continuous scene is also
genuinely more impressive to an employer than a 60-second montage.

## LEARN
- Continuity under real production pressure
- The 180° line as a constraint you actually obey when it is inconvenient

## WATCH / READ
`projects/controlled-micro-scene.md` — 15 min. Nothing else.

## DO
1. **(30 min)** Shot list (3–4 rows, all columns) into the Console. **Floor plan sketch with the
   180° line drawn.** Do not skip the sketch.
2. **(60 min)** Start and end frames for every shot. **At least one shot's start frame must come
   through ComfyUI Workflow B** — that is a hard requirement of the brief.
3. **(90 min)** Generate all shots at rung ≥2. Iterate at 480p fast tier.
   **Maximum 25 generations total.** Shot QC each one as it lands.
4. **(45 min)** One AE pass on the weakest shot (repair or integration).
5. **(45 min)** Assemble in Premiere: cut, **at least one J or L cut**, continuous ambience bed
   + ≥3 designed effects, colour match across all shots, export.
6. **(30 min)** Film QC. Naive-viewer test.

## VERIFY
- [ ] A stranger can describe what happened — ask one, say nothing first
- [ ] They can sketch the floor plan afterwards (the location reads as one place)
- [ ] Screen direction consistent — log direction per shot and check adjacencies
- [ ] The Comfy-controlled shot's geometry matches its control input
- [ ] Every shot Shot-QC **PASS**
- [ ] Film QC ≥ 60/100, no criterion below 3
- [ ] ≤ 25 generations

## SHIP
`09_master/micro_scene_v1.mp4` + sidecars + generation log + completed Film QC sheet.

## FAILURE CONDITIONS
- **The scene feels like three unrelated clips** → geography was never established. Shot 1 or 2
  must show where things are. This is a boarding failure, not a generation failure.
- **You want to use a beautiful shot that breaks the 180° line** → **don't.** This is the
  specific discipline the project exists to build. A plain shot that respects geography beats a
  beautiful one that breaks it, every time.
- **Blew past 25 generations** → you are re-rolling instead of re-specifying. Check the
  abandonment rule.

## RECOVERY
Behind schedule: cut to 3 shots. Three shots that hold together beat four that don't.
A shot that keeps failing: change its size or angle so the failing element is out of frame.

## STOP RULE
Stop when Film QC ≥60 and the naive-viewer test passes. **Phase 2 is complete. Take stock:**
you are at ~50 hours and you have two finished pieces. That is on pace.
