# DAY 13 — Workflow A and the Previs→Video Bridge

**TIME BUDGET: 5h** — 0.5h watch · 0.5h read · 3.5h do (incl. 1h Tool Track) · 0.5h QC

## OUTCOME
**`previs_depth_to_cinematic.json`** working and exported, plus a proven Genjutsu motion-transfer
result. This is the day the whole pipeline connects end to end.

## WHY THIS MATTERS
Today satisfies two of the final film's hard technique requirements simultaneously: "≥1 shot
using 3D/previs control" and "≥1 shot using ComfyUI structural control". It is also the most
technically impressive thing you will build, and the centrepiece of your portfolio breakdown.

## LEARN
- Depth ControlNet: strength as the dial between "obeys geometry" and "interprets loosely"
- The inversion at the heart of this course: **you specify composition; the model supplies look**
- Genjutsu (`hf_mult_motion_control`) — motion transfer from a driving video onto reference images
- LLM structured output for shot-spec expansion

## WATCH

## READ

## DO
1. **(60 min) TOOL TRACK — Console v3.** LLM structured-output expansion: one-line shot
   description → full validated `ShotSpec`. **Ensure an invalid model response is caught and
   reported, not silently written.** Use it to draft specs for the final film.
2. **(90 min) EXERCISE C3 — build Workflow A.**
   ```
   depth pass (from Day 12) → ControlNet Apply (Depth, strength 0.6–0.9)
   checkpoint + CLIP (+/-)  → KSampler (SEED FIXED) → VAE Decode → Save
   ```
   **The exit test: same depth map + same seed + three different prompts → three images whose
   GEOMETRY is identical and whose CONTENT differs.** That is the entire point of the module.
3. **(30 min) EXERCISE C5 — strength sweep.** Run at ControlNet strength 0.3 / 0.6 / 0.9, same
   seed. Build a labelled 3-up. Write one sentence on where control breaks down.
   **This goes in the portfolio breakdown — it demonstrates methodology, not luck.**
4. **(45 min) Genjutsu bridge (Route 3).** `hf_mult_motion_control` with
   `video_references` = your Day 12 dolly clip, `image_references` = your locked hero design.
   Compare against the plain `video_references` result from Day 12.
5. **(15 min)** Export `workflows/A_depth2cine_v1.json` + README. Commit.

## FAILURE CONDITIONS
- **Depth pass appears to do nothing** → in order: (1) **FLUX/ControlNet architecture mismatch**,
  (2) depth inverted, (3) strength below ~0.4. Check all three before assuming the workflow is wrong.
- **Geometry matches but images are ugly** → correct behaviour. Geometry is the win; look is a
  prompt problem. Do not lower ControlNet strength to make it prettier — you would be discarding
  the control you came for.
- **Genjutsu output ignores your motion** → your driving clip may be too short, too fast, or too
  ambiguous. Grey-box motion needs to be legible; simplify it.

## RECOVERY
If Workflow A won't come together, Route 2 (plain `video_references`) still satisfies the
"3D/previs control" requirement for the final film. Log Workflow A as incomplete and move on —
Day 14 is cuttable if you need the time to come back to this. **This is worth more than Unreal.**

## STOP RULE
Stop when the geometry-lock test passes. That test *is* the skill.
