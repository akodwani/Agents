## Script (306 words)

> The account's running at a hundred and five dollars a paid start against a ninety dollar target. Six of the ten tests missed it. But the useful thing isn't which ones lost — it's that the four ads with the best attention and the four with the best conversion don't share a single row. Zero overlap.
>
> The trend remix got fifty percent thumbstop, best in the test, and converted trial-to-paid at eleven and a half percent — worst in the test. The screen-record demo got thirty-nine, converted at twenty-one, and came in at sixty-two dollars a paid start.
>
> So attention isn't the problem. It's being bought from the wrong people.
>
> Here's what I think is going on. The ad sets the expectation the trial has to live up to. The skit promised a joke and delivered software — fourteen percent paid. The demo promised exactly what the app does — twenty-one. The before-and-after promised a transformation — nearly twenty-seven, best in the set. Trial-to-paid gets decided before anyone clicks.
>
> Which gives you one rule. Show the transformation, don't describe it. The five ads where the product's output is on screen came in at seventy-seven dollars a paid start. The five where somebody talks about it, a hundred and ninety-three. Almost identical thumbstop. The whole difference is after the click.
>
> The pain that tested hardest is the ten minutes after a call, when the decisions are still in your head and nothing's written down. Two ads aimed at that moment got the highest thumbstop in the account.
>
> So — three scripts. A screen record on TikTok, off C05. A Meta piece about a voice-memo backlog nobody ever played back, off C09 and C01. And a twenty-second before-and-after in a hallway, off C07 — that one ran on Shorts, and I'm porting it because it converted best and the mechanism travels.
>
> All three open on the mess, not the clean version. All three end on a number that belongs to the person holding the phone, not a statistic.
>
> Next test is the hook: dread-first against proof-first. I'd kill anything past ninety dollars at five hundred spent, and put the budget behind whichever one holds under eighty.
>
> On AI — I used Claude for the arithmetic, for research, and to argue against my own drafts. The calls are mine.
