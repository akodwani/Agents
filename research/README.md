## The short version

**Primary lane: performance/growth creative strategist**, not AI artist. It is the only lane in
the census where his differentiator (paid ad-revenue economics literacy + ability to generate the
creative himself) sits on the axis the lane screens on, and its entry comp at a named NYC
exemplar is $90,800–$113,500 against a $50k floor.

**Secondary: AI-enabled marketing/content**, at 6–8 additional hours, for breadth and interview
reps.

**Blocked:** AI Artist as employment (NYC reqs demand 5+ years), junior-under-a-CD (no book, no
Adobe, no network, contracting rung), creative technologist as employment (3–6 year floor — but
revived as a zero-cost freelance side-channel).

**The constraint that shaped every recommendation:** his committed slate already consumes
~91–134 of ~130 available hours. Real discretionary budget is ~15–20 hours, not the 120 the
brief allowed. So nothing here is "additionally build X" — it re-shapes committed work, and is
funded by one deliberate cut (finish the master cut in CapCut, defer DaVinci Resolve to 2027).

**Start in week 1:** the self-funded Meta creative test. Low-budget campaigns need 4–6 weeks
minimum before they read, and without owned spend numbers the primary lane has no proof object.

## Evidence discipline

Three labels used throughout: **[V]** verified-in-search-index · **[C]** aggregator count-only
(admissible for counts only) · **[M]** modeled, with assumptions shown inline. No percentage
anywhere in this project is unlabeled. Where nothing was found, the files say
"no representative data found" — most notably for named traced entrants and for every channel
conversion rate.

**Method limitation, stated up front:** this environment's network egress policy blocked all
direct page fetches (`phase-1.md` §0). Every source below was surfaced and excerpted through the
search index; none was read on-page. Postings cited as [V] were index-verified for employer,
title, and requirement text, but were not confirmed still-live.
