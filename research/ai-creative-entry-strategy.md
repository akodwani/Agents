## Executive Summary

You are not getting silence because you lack talent. You are getting silence because **eight applications is not an experiment** — for your profile the arithmetic says zero responses is the ~92% likely outcome even if nothing were wrong. Nothing has been measured.

That's the comfortable half. The uncomfortable half: **scaling that channel is also wrong.** You'd need ~70 applications for a coin-flip at one reply, into postings that mostly demand 2–4 years, at employers cutting headcount — WPP is cutting hundreds more through 2026, Omnicom 4,000 post-IPG, and 91% of senior US agency leaders expect AI to shrink their staff. The holdco junior rung is closing while you knock on it.

Your portfolio is **below bar, but by less than it feels.** The gap isn't quality — your 4-character face-identity system with a 46-asset reference library is precisely the "repeatable workflow" evidence hiring managers say is missing from most portfolios. The gap is **shape and venue**: zero pieces that play start to finish, zero real-brand anchors, and it all lives on **GitHub Pages**, which reads as *engineer*, not *artist*. That miscategorization is already costing you — the one flagship application you made was to WPP Hex, a creative *technologist* program, the exact lane you'd ruled out.

The one clean junior hire I could trace: **Victor Moreno**, hired as an AI Artist at UNCUT off a **Nike spec ad with ~2,000 views in week one**, with **ComfyUI named as the deciding factor**. Not viral. Not an audience. One finished spec piece for a real brand, on a video platform.

Ninety days will not produce a full-time creative job — that's a 6–18 month arc and pretending otherwise costs you months. Ninety days can produce **first paid work, a portfolio at bar, and live conversations with people who hire.** Keep the DIRECTV job: it funds the runway, and knowing how ad revenue actually works is the rarest thing you own.

## 1. Diagnosis (ranked)

**1. Sample size — the zero is uninformative. (~95% certain; it's arithmetic.)**
At a realistic 1% per-application response rate for a career-changer with no creative credits, 8 applications yield 0.08 expected responses. P(zero) ≈ 92%. You'd need ~69 applications for even odds of one reply, ~300 to be confident. *This explains the past and nothing about the future.*

**2. Wrong channel. (~80% — and this is the actionable cause.)**
Every named success in the evidence was discovered through published finished work, never an application. The market's own hiring managers describe the mechanism explicitly. Applications are ~1/242. Referrals are 30–50% of US hires from 7% of applicants, with a 4x offer multiplier.

**3. Portfolio format and venue below bar. (~75%.)**
Zero finished start-to-finish pieces. Zero real-brand anchors. Hosted on GitHub Pages as written case studies, in a market that hires off finished video on video platforms.

**4. Resume/ATS mismatch. (~65%, but only where ATS exists.)**
90%+ of employers screen by ATS. *KeyBank analyst → Ad Sales Revenue Ops* has no keyword surface for a creative req. Note the asymmetry: this bites hardest exactly where you were applying (WPP) and barely at all at the small shops where a founder reads the inbox.

**5. Wrong role targets. (~55%.)**
Hex is 16 seats per cycle, globally, for the technologist lane you de-scoped. Most posted roles want 2–4 years. Holdcos are in net contraction.

**The single cause the evidence most supports:** the literal cause is **sample size**; the cause that will keep it zero is **channel**. You built an engineering-shaped artifact and pushed it through the lowest-yield channel in the market, at a volume too small to learn from.

### Answering your specific questions

- **Is a self-created brand read as weaker than any external brand, even unpaid?** **Yes.** Victor Moreno's hire object was a *Nike* spec ad. Inventing the brand removes the only thing a viewer can calibrate against — you control the brief, the tone, and the rules, so you cannot fail. A spec ad for a brand everyone knows is a test you can fail in public. That's why it counts.
- **Is Higgsfield-only a filter-out signal?** **Not by name** — Higgsfield appears in genuine job postings (e.g. a $1,000 fixed-price Upwork role listing "Sora / ComfyUI / Higgsfield / OpenArt," 2026-02-19). But **Higgsfield-*only* is.** Postings enumerate 4–6 tools. And Higgsfield is an agentic layer that runs the pipeline for you; ComfyUI is evidence you understand the pipeline. Employers are buying the second thing.
- **Is the absence of a reel itself the problem?** **Largely yes — but don't make a reel.** A montage from someone with no professional credits is weak. Both traceable cases were *one complete piece*.
- **Is zero social reach the problem?** **No.** This is the comfortable explanation and the evidence rejects it. Victor Moreno was hired at ~2K views. Reach is a distribution mechanism, not a credential.

### The two upgrades that most change your odds

**Upgrade 1 — Ship one 45–60 second spec ad for a real brand: fully finished, graded, sound-designed, on YouTube and Instagram.**
Pick a brand adjacent to what you already have (streetwear/footwear/beverage — you have a locked visual language and 4 consistent characters, so reuse the system rather than starting over). Cut the case study to three lines under the video. The video is the artifact; the writing is a footnote.

**Upgrade 2 — Move your portfolio off GitHub Pages.**
Get a real domain and a video-first landing page, or make a YouTube/Instagram profile the front door. Keep the GitHub site as a "process" link for anyone who asks. Right now your URL is telling creative hiring managers you're an engineer, and you have been believing it too.

*(Third, sequenced behind these: start ComfyUI at week 4. It was the stated deciding factor in the one junior hire I could trace.)*

## 4. The 90-Day Sequence

### Weeks 1–2 — Ship the spec piece
- **Day 1:** Pick the brand. Real, recognizable, adjacent to your existing visual language. Write a one-line brief you could hand a client.
- **Day 1:** Register a domain. Video-first page. GitHub Pages demoted to a "process" link.
- **Days 2–10:** Produce the 45–60s spot. Storyboard → generate → **edit → grade → sound design.** The last three are where the bar is; budget half your time there. If CapCut can't grade it, learn DaVinci Resolve's free tier — it's a weekend, not a semester.
- **Day 3 (parallel, 30 min):** Register for **Higgsfield Earn**. Submit to an open campaign that week. 90% approval rate, weekly ~$100k pool, open to creators of any size. This is your fastest route to a *paid brand credit*, not to income.
- **Days 11–14:** Publish to YouTube + Instagram. Rewrite your LinkedIn headline to lead with the work, not the ops title.

### Weeks 3–4 — Turn it on
- Post the piece and 2–3 process cuts (behind-the-scenes of the character system — this is your differentiated content, almost nobody else can show it).
- **Upwork live.** Position as *"performance creative for advertisers — AI video by someone who reads the campaign P&L."* Not "AI video guy." 5–8 proposals/week, targeting the **$150–$1,200/deliverable** band. Refuse the $20 tier.
- **First 10 outreach messages** (template below). Targets: Collier Simon, Genre.ai, Wonder Studios, Utopai, plus 6 NYC independents standing up AI video units.
- **Week 4:** ComfyUI starts. One hour a week, background thread.
- **The DIRECTV conversation.** Find whoever owns creative/ad products. Not "can I have a job" — *"I've been making AI video on the side, here's a spot I cut. I hear we're doing more with generative creative. Can I help with anything?"* You are the cheapest resource they will ever be offered.

### Weeks 5–8 — Volume and conversion
- **Second spec piece.** Different brand, different register (if piece 1 was atmospheric, make this one funny — *"if you make funny spec commercials, they will hire you"*). Use ComfyUI for at least one component so you can say you did.
- Upwork: 8–10 proposals/week. Take the first gig even if underpriced — the first review is worth more than the fee.
- Outreach: 10/week, cumulative 50+. Always artifact-first.
- Attend one **Curious Refuge NYC meetup**. In-person converts to referral; referral is the 4x multiplier.
- Keep submitting to Higgsfield Earn campaigns — they're real briefs from real brands, handed to you free.

### Weeks 9–12 — Compound
- **Third piece**, or first real client work becomes the portfolio piece (client work outranks spec, always).
- Convert one client to a small retainer conversation. **$1,500–$8,000/mo** is the tier where this replaces income; a single $2,000/mo retainer plus the day job is a credible bridge.
- Apply — selectively, ~3 total — to genuinely junior postings: freelance-to-hire listings (an "AI Artist — freelance to permanent" role in NYC is a real, posted structure), Curious Refuge jobs board, independents. Not holdcos.
- Reassess the DIRECTV internal path with 90 days of evidence behind you.

### Outreach template (artifact-first; personalization roughly doubles reply rate)

> Subject: spec spot for [Brand] — 52 seconds
>
> [Name] — I made this on spec for [Brand]: [link]
>
> Consistent character identity across the whole spot using a locked reference system I built (46 assets, 4 characters, swappable wardrobe). Graded and sound-designed. Solo, [X] days.
>
> Day job is ad sales revenue ops at DIRECTV Advertising, so I know what a campaign costs and why creative variants under-deliver — unusual combination for someone making the assets.
>
> Saw [specific thing they shipped]. If you ever need overflow on AI video, I'd like to be on that list.
>
> — [name]

Rules: lead with the link; never attach a resume; one specific reference to their actual work; ask for the overflow list, not a job. Generic AI-written outreach actively damages trust in 2026 — send 10 real ones over 100 templated ones.

**Tier 1 — independents & small AI-native (founder reads the inbox):** Collier Simon · Genre.ai · Wonder Studios · Utopai Studios · Agency Within · NYC independent shops standing up AI video units
**Tier 2 — AI-native studios (senior-weighted; play the long game):** Promise · Asteria · Secret Level · Toonstar · Invisible Universe · Pigeon Shrine · TCL Studios · Netflix INKubator

## 5. Leading Indicators and the Pivot Rule

**By Day 30 you should see:**
- One finished spec piece live on a video platform ✓ (fully in your control — if this is missing, nothing else in this report matters)
- At least one Higgsfield Earn payout approved
- 1–3 Upwork proposal replies out of ~20
- 1–2 replies from ~20 outreach messages
- Portfolio off GitHub Pages
- One conversation started internally at DIRECTV

**By Day 60:**
- Two finished pieces
- **First paid client work delivered** (any amount)
- 3–5 live conversations with people who could hire or refer
- One repeat client or a second gig from the same source
- ComfyUI: one piece with a ComfyUI component in it

**Pivot rules:**
- **No spec piece live by Day 30** → the problem is execution capacity, not the market. Cut scope to 30 seconds and ship. Everything downstream is blocked on this.
- **Piece live, zero outreach replies from 40+ messages by Day 45** → the work is below bar. Get brutal external critique (post it for feedback in the Higgsfield/CR communities, ask specifically about finishing quality) before sending message 41.
- **Replies but no paid work by Day 60** → pricing or positioning. Drop your rate once, deliberately, to buy the first review, then raise it.
- **Paid work but nothing above $300/deliverable by Day 90** → you're in the commodity tier. Stop competing on generation; rebuild the pitch entirely around the advertiser-economics wedge.
- **Day 90, none of the above** → the honest read is that 10 hrs/week isn't enough while the day job drains you. The answer is *more concentrated hours* (a two-week PTO sprint), not quitting. Nothing in this evidence supports leaving DIRECTV inside 90 days.

## 6. Honest Odds

| Outcome (by Day 90) | Probability | Base rate behind it |
|---|---|---|
| **(a) Full-time creative role** | **5–8%** | External FT with zero creative credits, into reqs demanding 2–4 yrs, in a market cutting headcount. Typical arc is 6–18 months. The DIRECTV internal move is the best single shot and adds maybe 3–5 points — but would likely land creative-*adjacent*, not pure creative |
| **(b) First paid AI-creative work** | **~80%** | Higgsfield Earn alone: 90% approval, weekly $100k pool, open to any size — likely inside 2–5 weeks. Requires only that you submit |
| **(b′) First paid work from a real external client** (stricter) | **50–60%** | Upwork AI video +329% YoY, but requires the portfolio piece to exist plus ~40 proposals |
| **(c) Neither** | **15–20%** | Concentrated almost entirely in the scenario where the spec piece never ships |

**Read the split honestly.** (a) is low and no amount of effort inside 90 days moves it much — that's the market, not you. (b) is high and mostly under your control. The dominant risk in (c) isn't the market rejecting you; it's a draining day job eating the 10 hours. Your 3-month deadline is achievable if you define it as *credible path in motion* — which is what you actually said you needed. Redefined as *signed full-time offer*, it is not, and someone should tell you that now rather than on day 89.
