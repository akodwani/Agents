## 1.1 The application channel: is it functioning?

### Application volume is the dominant fact of the 2026 market

- The average job opening now receives **~242 applications**; Glassdoor's corporate average is ~250 per role. ([The Interview Guys, 2026](https://blog.theinterviewguys.com/the-average-job-opening-now-gets-242-applications/))
- LinkedIn reports roughly **11,000 applications submitted per minute**, a **45% year-over-year increase**, attributed directly to candidates using generative AI to draft and submit faster. ([Clever CV summary of LinkedIn data, 2026](https://www.clever.cv/blog/ai-job-application-flood-how-to-stand-out-2026); [CNBC, 2026-01-11](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/01/11/ai-dominate-hiring-2026-linkedin-execs-top-tips-stand-out.html))
- Hiring-side commentary specific to creative/design roles: "A single posting can pull in hundreds of well-written, similar-sounding applications, and almost none of them tell you whether the person behind it can actually do the job." ([Authentic Jobs, "Hiring Developers and Designers in the Age of AI: What Actually Works in 2026"](https://authenticjobs.com/hiring-developers-and-designers-in-the-age-of-ai/))
- Same source, on juniors: hiring managers "are being inundated with hundreds of applications for roles, and they all get automatically rejected, based on sheer inability to tell them apart."

**Read:** the cold-application channel is not *closed*, but its per-application yield is roughly 1/242 before any quality filter. For a candidate with no creative credential to sort on, effectively worse.

### Entry-level specifically is contracting

- US entry-level hiring fell **6% year-over-year** between Dec 2025 and Feb 2026. (cited in [Authentic Jobs, 2026](https://authenticjobs.com/hiring-developers-and-designers-in-the-age-of-ai/))
- NACE's April 2026 Spring Update lists the top entry-level-hiring sectors for the Class of 2026 as Information, Engineering Services, Wholesale Trade, Construction, and Misc. Professional Services. **Creative/advertising is not on the list.** ([summarized via Effective Hiring, 2026](https://www.effectivehiring.com/blog/what-small-employers-can-learn-from-new-graduate-hiring-data-in-2026))
- Graduates are "applying for more jobs while receiving fewer offers." (same)

### Referral share

- Employee referrals account for **30–50% of all US new hires** while being only **~7% of applicants**; referred candidates are ~**4x** more likely to receive an offer; referral hires close in ~22 days vs ~37 days. ([ERIN, 2026](https://erinapp.com/blog/enterprise-employee-referral-statistics-you-need-to-know-for-2026/); [Interview Guys, 2026](https://blog.theinterviewguys.com/referred-candidates-get-hired-4x-more-often-so-stop-asking-for-a/))
- Caveat worth keeping: the same sources note average companies run 5–15% referral share and only top performers hit 20–35%. The "hidden job market" framing is **often overstated** — one source says so explicitly. Do not over-claim here.

**Read:** referral advantage is real and large (4x), but the "80% of jobs are never posted" trope is not supported. The honest version: *posted roles exist, but the posted channel is where you compete against 242 people and the referral channel is where you compete against ~3.*

## 1.2 WHO actually hires — mapped

### A. Holdcos / big agencies — **net cutting, not net hiring**

This is the single most important structural fact for this candidate.

- **WPP**: another round of layoffs, hundreds of jobs globally before end of 2026. ([Ad Age, 2026](https://adage.com/agencies/aa-wpp-plans-hundreds-more-layoffs-by-end-of-2026/))
- **Omnicom**: absorbed IPG Nov 2025, announced **4,000 post-merger layoffs** on top of 8,200+ pre-merger cuts across the two companies; cost-savings target doubled to $1.5B with **$1B from labor**. ([MarketScreener/FT, 2025-11](https://in.marketscreener.com/news/omnicom-to-cut-4-000-jobs-shut-several-agencies-after-ipg-takeover-ft-reports-ce7d51d8db8ff527); [eMarketer, 2026](https://www.emarketer.com/content/omnicom-s-latest-report-signals-agency-shift-human-talent-ai))
- Industry-wide: **150,000+ announced job cuts citing AI since early 2025**; **91% of US senior agency leaders expect AI to reduce headcount**. ([AI CERTs News, 2026](https://www.aicerts.ai/news/marketing-agency-layoffs-surge-as-wpp-pursues-ai-efficiency-drive/))
- Direct statement on juniors: holding companies are "largely shifting focus to enhancing AI capabilities rather than employing new and emerging talent." (same)

**Volume at the one holdco program the candidate applied to:** WPP's Hex studio inside WPP Production is ~**50 creative technologists total**. The associated Creative Tech Apprenticeship recruits **16 people per cycle**, is nine months, paid, and explicitly targets "talent from outside traditional advertising and engineering pipelines." ([WPP, 2024-07](https://www.wpp.com/en/news/2024/07/wpp-seeks-next-generation-of-creative-technologists-for-2025-apprenticeship-programme); [ContentGrip, on Hex](https://www.contentgrip.com/wpp-hex-studio/); [LBBOnline](https://lbbonline.com/news/WPP-Launches-Creative-Technology-Apprenticeship))

→ **16 seats globally, from a self-selected applicant pool of exactly the people who read WPP press releases.** This is a lottery, not a channel. Applying was not a mistake; treating it as a plan is.

Also note: Hex is a *creative technologist* pipeline — the lane the candidate explicitly de-scoped. He applied to the thing he said he doesn't want.

### D. Traditional agencies bolting on AI units — **the most junior-accessible pocket found**

- **Collier Simon** (independent full-service agency) is building "an AI-first video studio inside the agency," explicitly for "high-quality AI video for ads, social content." Posting emphasizes "real filmmaking instincts (story, pacing, composition)" and knowing "how to translate those instincts into AI video production."
- **Agency Within** posting AI-adjacent roles on Greenhouse.
- A "**AI Artist — freelance to permanent role**" posting in **New York, NY** exists on CareerBuilder — direct evidence that *freelance-to-hire is a named, posted structure in this niche in NYC*.

Independents are the pocket where a junior can actually land, because they hire in ones and twos and the founder reads the inbox.

### E. Freelance marketplace layer

Observed posted rates in this band: **$50–90 per video with 3–4 day turnaround** for AI video ad/UGC work; ZipRecruiter aggregate for "AI Art" jobs shows a **$36k–$75k** band (vs. $127k–$200k for "AI Artist," which is a different, senior population). Treat aggregator salary figures as weak evidence (per research discipline rules, job-board data is admissible for counts, not for market narrative).

## 1.3 Does visible social presence function as a de facto requirement?

This is the strongest, cleanest finding in Phase 1, and it comes from **operators, not career blogs**.

**Direct hiring-manager statement — PJ Accetturo, founder/CEO of Genre.ai** (an AI creative studio that actually employs AI creatives, clients Oracle/Popeyes/Qatar Airways):

> "**If you make great spec commercials, brands will find you. If you make funny spec commercials, they will hire you.**"

> "The people who can think like directors and storytellers no longer need permission from a studio, a crew, or a massive budget. Underdogs can disrupt the space now."
> ([The Media Brain interview](https://themediabrain.substack.com/p/ais-disruption-of-advertising-and); [Creative Boom](https://www.creativeboom.com/insight/how-ai-is-turning-the-ad-industry-on-its-head/))

Note the exact mechanism he describes: **spec commercials** (not personal-brand campaigns), and **inbound discovery** (not applications).

**The mechanism firing in public, with a named person — Liu Ziyu:**
- 29-year-old former **train driver** from Yunnan, China. No formal art or CS training; prior job was wedding photographer.
- Wrote/directed **"Zombie Scavenger,"** a 3.5-minute AI short, in **10 days** for **3,000 yuan (~$440)**, entirely with commercial AI tools.
- Posted to Douyin **2026-05-09**. Went viral.
- **PJ Accetturo saw it and publicly said: "This is one of the best short films I have seen in years… I would love to hire him but I cannot find him."** Liu emailed him; got a collaboration invitation; declined for personal reasons.
- ([SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/people-culture/china-personalities/article/3354882/china-ex-train-driver-creates-us440-short-film-receiving-hollywood-director-praise-job-offer); [Infomance](https://www.infomance.com/news/former-chinese-train-drivers-low-budget-ai-short-film-attracts-hollywood-interest/))

This is the cleanest possible proof of channel: **zero credentials + zero network + one finished narrative piece posted publicly → a studio CEO tries to hire you within weeks.** Note what was *not* involved: no application, no portfolio site, no case study, no degree, no agency contact.

**The scaled version — Zack London / "Gossip Goblin":**
- 35, LA, former anthropology student, no film-industry entry credential.
- Built **1M+ Instagram followers**, claims 500M+ total views.
- Result: THR called him "the George Lucas of AI filmmaking"; **theatrical release** of AI feature *Gods Don't Give Gifts* in limited US theaters Oct 30.
- ([THR](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/gossip-goblin-zack-london-ai-films-watch-1236544634/); [IndieWire](https://www.indiewire.com/news/breaking-news/gossip-goblin-releasing-ai-feature-film-theaters-1235206317/); [THR on X](https://x.com/THR/status/2050267842625605706))

**Counter-evidence on the "reach = requirement" framing:** Liu Ziyu had no prior following — the *work* traveled, not the account. And Robert Gaudette won the $50,000 Grand Prix at Runway's AI Film Festival for "Face" and reportedly has **no desire to convert it into a studio job** ([THR](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-features/ai-films-face-mother-love-robert-gaudette-runway-1236624039/)). So: reach is not literally a checkbox requirement, but **public distribution of finished work is the discovery mechanism**, and an account with zero reach is a distribution system that doesn't run.

## 1.4 Phase 1 conclusions (to be attacked in Phase 5)

1. **The posted-application channel for junior AI-creative roles is technically open and practically dead** at this candidate's profile: ~242 applicants/posting, AI-inflated volume up 45% YoY, and holdcos in active headcount reduction rather than junior intake.
2. **The roles that are posted are mostly not junior.** Every specific posting surfaced wants 2–4 years (Wonder Studios) or mid-to-senior (Brandtech+ 100 roles). The genuinely junior structures are a 16-seat apprenticeship and freelance-to-hire postings.
3. **The named, verifiable success paths in this niche all run through published work, not applications.** Both named examples (Liu Ziyu, Zack London) were discovered because a finished piece was publicly distributed. The one direct hiring-manager quote available describes exactly this mechanism and names the artifact type: **spec commercials**.
4. **The highest-probability employer type for this candidate is not a holdco.** It is (a) independent agencies standing up AI video units, (b) AI-native shops small enough that the founder reads DMs, (c) freelance-to-hire postings, which demonstrably exist in NYC.
5. **THIN / unresolved going into Phase 2:** actual applicant counts on specific AI-creative postings; whether community Discords (Banodoco, Curious Refuge, Higgsfield) transact real gig flow; verified junior-hire portfolios.
