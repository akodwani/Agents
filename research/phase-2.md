# Phase 2 — The hiring bar: what a hired junior AI artist's portfolio actually looks like

**Research date:** 2026-08-17

## 2.0 Honest statement of sample quality — READ THIS FIRST

The brief asked for **10–15 verifiable people hired in the last ~18 months**. I found **four usable named cases and three senior counter-cases**. That is short of the target, and I am not going to pad it with course-marketing testimonials.

Why the sample is small, and why that is itself a finding:
- LinkedIn is not fetchable from this environment, and LinkedIn is where "I joined X" posts live.
- More importantly: **there are very few junior AI-creative hires to find.** The trade-press record of "AI studio hires person" is overwhelmingly *senior name from legacy Hollywood* (Christina Lee Storm, Paul Trillo, Promise's three executives). If a healthy junior intake pipeline existed, it would leave a public trace. It does not leave much of one.

Treat everything below as **directionally strong, statistically thin**.

## 2.1 The usable named cases

### Case 1 — Victor Moreno → AI Artist at UNCUT (Swedish film production company)
**The single most transferable case in the sample.** ([Curious Refuge](https://curiousrefuge.com/blog/how-to-get-hired-as-an-ai-artist))

| Dimension | What was true |
|---|---|
| Prior background | Career changer, non-traditional entry |
| Training | Curious Refuge AI Advertising (Caleb Ward, Dave Clark); AI Filmmaking workshops (Stacy Kelly); **free ComfyUI course (Mauricio Tonon)** |
| Portfolio centerpiece | **A Nike spec ad, published on YouTube.** ~2K views in week one |
| Decisive credential | **ComfyUI.** Stated explicitly: the ComfyUI course "was instrumental in helping him land his role at UNCUT, due to the company's highly technical profile" |
| Reach at hire | ~2K views. **Not viral. Not an audience.** |
| Format | Video on a video platform |

Four things this case establishes, and each of them cuts against the candidate:
1. **A spec ad for a REAL, recognizable brand** was the portfolio object — not a self-created brand.
2. **~2K views was enough.** Reach was *not* the mechanism. This is important and it is good news for the candidate: he does not need to go viral.
3. **Tool depth was the decisive filter**, and the specific tool was ComfyUI.
4. The work was **on YouTube**, not on a personal site.

## 2.2 The derived bar

Synthesizing the named cases with observed job-posting requirements:

**Posting requirements observed (Phase 1 sources):**
- Wonder Studios: **2–4 years** in a creative role at an agency/studio/content/media/tech org; "experience delivering professional-level work across demanding client campaigns."
- Brandtech+ 100 global roles: **mid-to-senior**.
- Collier Simon (independent, AI-first video studio): "real filmmaking instincts (story, pacing, composition)" translated into AI video production.
- Cross-posting tool stack: **Kling, Veo, Runway, Midjourney, Flux, ComfyUI** *plus* **Premiere Pro, After Effects, or DaVinci Resolve**. Higgsfield *does* appear by name in real postings (e.g. a $1,000 fixed-price Upwork "AI Generative Video Specialist (Sora / ComfyUI / Higgsfield / OpenArt)", posted 2026-02-19), but always as **one of several**, never alone.
- Portfolio-side: recruiters "explicitly looking for portfolios that include AI-augmented work **alongside classical creative shots**."
- Hiring-manager red flags: portfolios that "look like tool showcases rather than design work"; candidates who cannot explain brand consistency "beyond 'good prompts'"; **no evidence of repeatable workflows**. ([Hire Digital](https://hiredigital.com/hiring-guides/generative-ai-designer))

**The bar, stated plainly:**

| Dimension | The bar |
|---|---|
| Finished pieces | **1–3 pieces that play start-to-finish.** Not asset libraries. Liu: one 3.5-min short. Victor: one spec ad. |
| Brand anchor | At least one piece attached to a **real, recognizable external brand** (spec is fine — Victor's Nike ad was spec) |
| Finishing | Edit, sound design, grade. Both viral cases were fully finished pieces, not raw generations |
| Tool breadth | Multiple generators + a real NLE/compositor. **ComfyUI is the credential that reads as "technical depth"** in this market |
| Format | **Video, on a video platform** (YouTube / IG / TikTok / Douyin). Distribution is part of the artifact |
| Reach | **Not a requirement.** 2K views was sufficient in the one clean hire case |
| Process legibility | Must be able to explain the repeatable system, not "good prompts" |

## 2.3 Calibrating the candidate — dimension by dimension

| Dimension | Bar | Candidate | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Finished start-to-finish piece | 1–3 | **0.** A campaign of stills/assets, a Three.js app, a 3D model. No 45–60s finished film object | **Below** |
| Real-brand anchor | ≥1 spec for recognizable brand | **0.** Self-created streetwear brand | **Below** |
| Finishing craft | Graded, sound-designed | CapCut finishing only. No grade tooling, no sound design | **Below** |
| Tool breadth | Multi-generator + NLE/comp | Higgsfield only + CapCut. No ComfyUI, no LoRA, no AE/Premiere/Resolve/Nuke | **Below** |
| Format / venue | Video on video platform | **GitHub Pages site with written case studies** | **Below, and miscategorizing** |
| Reach | Not required | Zero | **At bar** (this is not his problem) |
| Systems / consistency thinking | Explain repeatable workflow | **Above bar.** 4 locked face-identity characters, swappable outfits, 46-asset reference system is *exactly* the "repeatable workflow" evidence hiring managers say is missing from most portfolios | **Above** |
| Commercial/business literacy | Rare | Ad sales revenue & expense ops at DIRECTV Advertising + finance rigor | **Well above — and completely unexploited** |

### The top 3 gaps, ranked by hiring impact

**Gap 1 — Format and venue. The work is invisible and mis-shelved.**
This is the biggest one and it is the cheapest to fix. Every discovery event in the evidence happened because finished *video* sat on a *video platform*. His work sits on **GitHub Pages**, which is where engineers put portfolios. A creative hiring manager who lands there reads "technologist," not "artist." Corroborating evidence that this miscategorization is real and is already costing him: **the one flagship application he made was to WPP Hex — a creative *technologist* apprenticeship — which is the lane he explicitly de-scoped.** His own portfolio told him what he was, and it told him wrong.

**Gap 2 — No finished, real-brand piece.**
Answering the brief's specific questions:
- *Is a self-created-brand campaign read as weaker than work for any external client/brand, even unpaid?* **Yes.** Victor Moreno's hire object was a **Nike** spec ad. A self-invented brand removes the only thing a viewer can calibrate against — it lets you move the goalposts on brief, tone, and brand rules, and hiring managers know it. A spec ad for a brand everyone knows is a test you can fail in public. That is why it counts.
- *Is the absence of a reel (vs. a website) itself the problem?* **Largely yes** — see Gap 1. But be precise: the fix is not "make a reel." A montage reel from someone with no professional credits is weak. The fix is **one complete piece**, which is what both viral cases actually were.

**Gap 3 — Tool-stack narrowness, specifically ComfyUI and a real finishing chain.**
- *Is Higgsfield-only a filter-out signal?* **Not by name** — Higgsfield is listed in genuine job postings. But **Higgsfield-only is**, for two reasons: (a) postings enumerate 4–6 tools and expect several, (b) in the one clean junior-hire case in the sample, **ComfyUI was the stated deciding factor**. Higgsfield is an agentic layer that does the pipeline for you; ComfyUI is evidence you understand the pipeline. Employers are buying the second thing.
- CapCut-only finishing is the quieter problem. "Graded and sound-designed" is table stakes for a piece meant to read as a commercial.

*Is zero social reach the problem?* **No.** This is the comfortable explanation and the evidence rejects it. Victor Moreno was hired off ~2K views. Reach is a *distribution mechanism*, not a credential. He needs a place to put work where a specific person can find it — not an audience.

## 2.4 Portfolio verdict

**Below the hiring bar — but the deficit is smaller and more fixable than it looks.**

The work demonstrates genuine capability. The character-consistency system is real craft and is the exact thing hiring managers complain is absent. But it is packaged as an **engineering artifact** (repo-hosted site, written case studies, systems documentation) for an audience that hires on **finished moving images**. He built the hard part and skipped the part that gets seen.

He is roughly **one 45–60 second piece away** from being at bar — provided that piece is for a real brand, is graded and sound-designed, and lives on YouTube/IG rather than on GitHub Pages.
